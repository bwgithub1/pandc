## Mary

### Jenny Cockell: The Woman That Reincarnated to See Her Children Again

<img class="intro-image" src="/nds/images/home/mary/image1.jpeg" alt="Jenny Cockell article image" />

In the 1950s, four-year-old Jenny Cockell from Northamptonshire, England, began to report memories of incidents, faces, family members, and places from what she believed was a past life.

Over time, those fragmented memories became more specific. She described herself as a mother of eight children living in Malahide, Ireland, who had died young and worried about her children because her husband was abusive and alcoholic.

Jenny said these memories were detailed enough that she could draw maps of the village while still a child. Later, she compared those memories to real maps and found that the village she remembered did exist.

As a child, she assumed everyone had memories from previous lives. During Sunday school discussions about life, death, and the afterlife, she became aware that others did not share those experiences.

As an adult in the 1970s, after starting her own family, she decided to investigate further and search for evidence. She later traveled to Ireland, visited Malahide, and identified places she believed she remembered, including the family house.

Her research led to records of a woman named Mary Sutton, a mother of eight who had died at that home. Jenny then placed a notice in a local newspaper to locate the children and eventually received information about them.

She learned that most of the children had been sent to orphanages, while one remained with the father. By searching orphanage records, she located six of the eight children; two had already passed away.

Jenny approached the family carefully and did not force any interpretation on them. Some accepted her as a reincarnation of their mother, while others viewed the connection differently, but they acknowledged details she shared that they believed only their mother could have known.
