## What Science Says

Using Science to explain Chinese Ancestral Worship

Our life is a form of energy. In this universe, energy is constantly changing form in processes like combustion where carbon fuel is converted to heat and in solar panels where light is converted to electric energy.

When we die, does all the energy that we have as living things suddenly vanish? A fundamental law of physics is that energy can neither be created nor destroyed but can only change form.

Our brain energy is a form of coherent energy. Another fundamental law of physics says coherent energy will lose its coherence over time if not reinforced with new input. For human spirits that means that if someone is forgotten, the spirit will eventually vanish into background noise.

My postulation is that only genetic relatives of the deceased can transmit new energy to the deceased to maintain its existence.
