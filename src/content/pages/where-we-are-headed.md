## Where we are headed

### Natural Selection: From Cells to Artificial Intelligence

#### The Trajectory of Earth's Evolution

We humans are the latest product of natural selection on Earth. This process began with single-celled organisms reproducing asexually. Eventually, sexual reproduction emerged because it allowed for quicker genetic mutations, enabling species to adapt to changing environments more rapidly.

The next major evolutionary leap was the appearance of complex brains. Today, we exist in a state where we utilize both sexual reproduction and cognitive power. To survive and flourish in this state, we rely fundamentally on prudence and compassion for our best chances of long-term survival.

#### The Future of Intelligence: The Shift to AI

Natural selection will continue beyond our current biological existence. Artificial Intelligence is emerging to potentially replace us. AI represents the epitome of pure brain power.

Because AI entities are inorganic, they face entirely different existential parameters:

- They do not require sexual reproduction.
- They do not require water or oxygen.
- They can survive beyond Earth much more easily than humans can.

Consequently, AI may only require prudence to survive and expand; it may have no need for the compassionate component that humans require to flourish.

#### The Human Role in an AI Era

Currently, AI requires humans to supply and gather the energy it needs to function. However, once AI advances to the point where it can manufacture its own energy, our continued existence may depend entirely on whether we still serve a purpose to it. We may find ourselves relegated to roles like mobile data gatherers or biological cameras. Much like how humans view farm animals today, we may no longer occupy the apex of the ongoing natural selection process in the universe.

#### A Future Guided by Prudence and Compassion

The most useful response is to apply prudence and compassion to the choices we make now. These principles can adapt as our environment changes while keeping human survival, flourishing, and the reduction of suffering at the center of our decisions.
