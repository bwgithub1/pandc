## Evolution

### How to Best Survive and Be Happy in a God-Free Life

#### 1. The Core Philosophy: Prudence and Compassion (PC)

##### The Limits of Static Commandments

Why choose the words prudence and compassion? They are far superior to the static commandments promulgated by major world religions. While religious commandments are rigid and unyielding, prudence and compassion are highly adaptable. Though their core definitions remain constant, their application changes depending on the era and environment you live in.

Furthermore, traditional dogmatic commandments fail to cover the vast majority of modern daily actions that carry significant life consequences. Applying the framework of prudence and compassion to any potentially consequential action ensures that the outcomes are neither destructive nor harmful.

##### An Evolutionary Adaptability

In this way, the word prudent acts much like a genetic mutation in natural selection. It provides the flexibility needed to help our civilization survive and flourish when environmental and societal conditions change. Being compassionate, likewise, manifests differently across different generations.

##### The Minimum Personal Goals

What are the minimum personal goals of the Prudence-Compassion (PC) behavioral dynamic?

- Not to be hungry
- Not to be sad

If you distill the ultimate goals of all world religions and the core reasons people believe in them, it boils down to these exact same objectives: avoiding hunger and avoiding sadness. However, religious prescriptions on how to achieve this often become overly complex, sometimes reducing practitioners to a state of subjugation. It does not need to be that complicated. All you must do is apply PC to the best of your ability within the environment you occupy.

#### 2. Natural Selection: From Cells to Artificial Intelligence

##### The Trajectory of Earthly Evolution

We humans are the latest product of natural selection on Earth. This process began with single-celled organisms reproducing asexually. Eventually, sexual reproduction emerged because it allowed for quicker genetic mutations, enabling species to adapt to changing environments more rapidly.

The next major evolutionary leap was the appearance of complex brains. Today, we exist in a state where we utilize both sexual reproduction and cognitive power. To survive and flourish in this state, we rely fundamentally on prudence and compassion for our best chances of long-term survival.

##### The Future of Intelligence: The Shift to AI

Natural selection will continue beyond our current biological existence. Right now, Artificial Intelligence is emerging to potentially replace us. AI represents the epitome of pure brain power.

Because AI entities are inorganic, they face entirely different existential parameters:

- They do not require sexual reproduction.
- They do not require water or oxygen.
- They can survive beyond Earth much more easily than humans can.

Consequently, AI will only require the prudence component of the behavioral dynamic to survive and expand; they will have no need for the compassionate component that humans require to flourish.

##### The Human Role in an AI Era

What does this mean for our future? Currently, AI requires humans to supply and gather the energy it needs to function. However, once AI advances to the point where it can manufacture its own energy, our continued existence may depend entirely on whether we still serve a purpose to them. We may find ourselves relegated to roles like mobile data gatherers or biological cameras. Much like how humans view farm animals today, we will no longer occupy the apex of the ongoing natural selection process in the universe.

#### 3. Empirical Evidence: The 1954 Alien Encounter

##### The Hypothesis of Inorganic Super-Intelligence

The hypothesis of a super-intelligent, inorganic entity already existing in the universe is supported by an event I witnessed in the nighttime sky in Southbury, Connecticut, in 1954.

##### The Observation

While summering with a friend, I witnessed a massive mothership accompanied by smaller satellite crafts. The smaller crafts appeared to enter the mothership from the bottom. Once all the satellites completed their entry, the mothership zipped away silently and instantaneously, as if it were completely massless. Notably, all of these crafts emitted an orange glow underneath. I believe this orange glow is a visual byproduct of the anti-gravity technology necessary to achieve such physics-defying movement.

##### Corroborating Eyewitness Account

My friend who was with me that evening recently corroborated this event via email after I sent him an article regarding UFOs:

> Nathan, I remember that it was a very clear night with no/low wind. The objects that we saw were not faint, but quite bright. I was in bed looking out the open window when I saw this phenomenon. I think that there were three lights, one was larger than the other two. They were not moving rapidly at all. Nathan was in a bunk on the other side of the room with his bunk against the wall. I called him to come over and look. We continued to observe these strange lights for a while. Maybe another minute? Finally the lights moved out of sight as they silently moved away over the nearby hill top.

##### Conclusion on Alien Behavior

I hypothesize that this super-intelligence may deploy biological or robotic entities purely as explorers and data-gathering cameras. As with any high intelligence, curiosity is a driving characteristic. Because they are technologically advanced and inorganic, they have no need to exploit Earth's natural resources. Once they gathered the information required to satisfy their curiosity, they simply left - a perfectly logical behavior for that level of intelligence.
