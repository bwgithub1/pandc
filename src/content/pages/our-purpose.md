## Our Purpose

Our purpose is not simply to survive, but to live in a way that is meaningful, ethical, and beneficial to others.

The document suggests that a purposeful life is built through virtue, compassion, care for family, and a willingness to seek wisdom beyond the self.

Prayer, belief, and reflection are all treated as tools for becoming more grounded and more humane in the face of life's uncertainties.

It is no accident that the two most populous nations in the world are dominated by beliefs that do not subscribe to a single all powerful deity.

Humankind took a great leap forward when they adopted monotheism, but based on all that I have learned about human history, adoption of monotheism was a huge step backwards for mankind in terms of tolerance for people of other beliefs.
