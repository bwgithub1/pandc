## Mary Meeting

This page records the Mary Sutton account described in the source text.

The story describes a woman in Ireland who died after childbirth and left behind six children. The account says she departed with a strong intention to keep helping and protecting them.

Years later, a young woman in the United States reported vivid memories and dreams that seemed connected to that Irish family. After researching those details, she reportedly reunited with Mary Sutton's children in Ireland.

In the narrative framework of this site, that reunion is treated as evidence of long-lived spirit connection, memory persistence, and ongoing care across generations.
